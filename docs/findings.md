There are nine quality issues across six applications, so 50% of records pass all eight defined quality rules. These are separate counts: fixing one issue does not necessarily make the application pass all rules.

Council Tax and Housing each directly use one application with expired-support technology. This describes the recorded mapping, not the severity of a live incident. The model does not infer indirect service impact.

## Proposed first actions

1. Assess the two applications with confirmed expired-support technology.
2. Assign owners to Permit Portal and Grants Manager, and obtain the missing cost and review date.
3. Update the overdue reviews, verify Archive Engine's support date and resolve Web Forms' service mapping.

No savings or operational improvements have been measured. Potential benefits should be evaluated after implementing changes, with a baseline and agreed follow-up measures.
